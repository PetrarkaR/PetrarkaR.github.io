## Something to play with

There's a square-root processor in [the lab]({{ '/lab/' | absolute_url }}).
You give it a number and watch it work through the instructions. No multiply
or divide instruction. Just shifts, subtraction, and moving bytes around.

Try 123. Then step through a few instructions instead of running the whole thing.
The result is 11, with a remainder of 2. Seeing the registers change makes the
algorithm a little less abstract.

## A question to take away

What changes inside the machine when the next pair of bits is brought in?

That's enough for one letter.
