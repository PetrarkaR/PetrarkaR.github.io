Adding two numbers

A bit more about it

The top row shows the carry leaving each bit. The bottom row shows whether each growing group generates a carry. After three combining stages, those groups give us all eight carries.

Both ways get the same answer. The tree does more work in parallel, at the cost of more wiring. These are logical stages, not clock cycles or measured hardware speed. The initial generate/propagate logic and final sum aren't counted in either row.

This little version has eight bits and a carry-in of zero. The ninth output bit is the carry-out.
